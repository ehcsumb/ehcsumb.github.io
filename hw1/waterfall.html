History: Waterfall Development

Origin

Royce’s 1970 paper is generally considered to be the paper which defined the stagewise “waterfall” model of the software process. Wikipedia

Royce pictured the waterfall model with the following seven steps: Systems requirements, Software requirements, Analysis, Program design, Coding, Testing, and Operation. Wikipedia

The twist: Royce warned against it

Directly under his diagram, Royce wrote: “I believe in this concept, but the implementation described above is risky and invites failure.” Royce, 1970, original paper

He also wrote that “in my experience, the simpler method has never worked on large software development efforts”. Wheeler, quoting Royce

Many people just looked at the easiest-to-read pretty figures in the paper, and didn’t notice the text that explained why the waterfall model was risky. David A. Wheeler

Where it still fits

Waterfall can be beneficial for projects with well-defined and stable requirements, where a clear roadmap is essential for success. LaunchNotes
