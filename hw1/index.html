What is Agile?

Agile is defined by the Agile Manifesto. It is a document produced by 17 developers at Snowbird, Utah in 2001. This document consists of 4 Agile Values and 12 Agile Principles. agilemanifesto.org

Two of the four values: Individuals and interactions over processes and tools. Working software over comprehensive documentation. TechTarget

The Manifesto forms the foundation for popular agile methods like Scrum, Kanban, and Extreme Programming. PW Skills

How widely it’s used

The 17th State of Agile Report found that 71% Use Agile in their SDLC. Business Wire / Digital.ai

Scrum is the most popular Agile methodology — 63% of Agile teams use Scrum methodology. Plaky, citing State of Agile

The top five factors driving agile adoption are “Prioritize, deliver, and measure incremental customer and business value,” “Accelerate time to market,” “Digital transformation,” “Delivery predictability,” and “Better managing distributed teams.” CSU Ohio Pressbooks

Main Agile Frameworks

Scrum insists that teams comprise three distinct roles — the product owner, the ScrumMaster and dev team members. TPXimpact

The key difference between Kanban and Scrum is that Kanban is continuous, while Scrum is iterative. DZone

XP’s practices include Test Driven Development, Customer Testing, Continuous Integration, Small Releases and Pair Programming. TPXimpact
