Agile Today Waterfall DevOps Comparison

Comparison between the three

Sources for the framing:

Agile is flexible and iterative, emphasizing continuous feedback and adaptation. Waterfall is sequential and rigid, with distinct phases completed in order. Forecast

Agile is about process and people in software development, whereas DevOps is about process and tools across software delivery and operations. Graphite

Agile’s cadence is iterative, whereas DevOps is continuous flow. Graphite

Waterfall Agile DevOps
Began 1970 (Royce) 2001 (Manifesto) 2009 (DevOpsDays)
Flow Phases done once, in order Repeating sprints Continuous pipeline
Who collaborates Separate teams hand off work Developers + customer/product Developers + operations
Testing Near the end Every sprint Automated, constantly
Releases One big release Every few weeks Frequent, even daily
Best for Stable, fixed requirements Changing requirements Services updated often
